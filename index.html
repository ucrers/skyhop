How to play

  • Drag anywhere on the screen to steer the orb left and right. It bounces automatically.
  • Collect sparks to charge your dash. When it's full, double-tap to blast upward.
  • Grab power-ups: the shield saves you from one fall, the rocket flies you up fast.
  • Watch out for fragile platforms – they break after one bounce.

Frequently asked questions

How do I turn the sound off?
Use the sound button in the main menu. The game also respects your iPhone's silent switch.

Can I reset my best score?
Your scores are stored only on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app resets them.

Does the game need an internet connection?
No. Skyhop Orb works completely offline.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Skyhop Orb ("the app") is developed by Ersin Ucar. Your privacy matters, so the app is built to collect nothing.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your best and last score and your sound setting locally on your iPhone so they're there next time you play. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, advertising or analytics services.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects no data from children either. It is suitable for all ages.
